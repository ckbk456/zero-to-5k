# Toward 10K — 12-stage second-program proposal

Researched 2026-10-19. **Proposal only; not enabled in the app.** The product owner requested a second-stage plan. A qualified race-walking coach should review the doses, entry criteria and technique before implementation. This is original product design informed by general guidance, not a validated race-walking protocol.

## Entry and outcome

Start after comfortable, repeatable 40-minute race-walking practice, with normal recovery before the next session and no unresolved symptoms affecting gait. One maximal 40-minute effort is not the intended starting point. Consolidate earlier stages when needed. These are practical prompts rather than validated clinical screening cutoffs.

Build to 80 continuous minutes. That equals 10 km at 8:00/km; the app must never infer distance or legal technique from time. Allow up to 90 minutes including bookends. No pace requirement, speed work, mandatory GPS or distance-based completion.

## Schedule

Three nonconsecutive sessions a week, at a conversational effort. Each session adds five minutes of ordinary walking before and after the main block. The table shows **race-walking main-block minutes only**.

The shorter sessions stay familiar while the long session grows. Stages 4 and 8 reduce the long session; stage 11 repeats 75 minutes before 80. Final scheduled weekly time is 180 minutes including bookends. These consolidation doses are design choices, not experimentally established recovery requirements.

New longest-session records rise by five minutes, but adjacent long sessions can rise by ten minutes following a lighter stage. Neither measure is a safety threshold. Extending a continuous session raises both target duration and longest bout; no one-variable progression claim applies.

## Repeat and step back

- Keep sessions in order; do not cram missed days together.
- Shorten a session or use ordinary-walking breaks when effort or technique feels beyond control. Repeat the last manageable stage after recovery.
- Interrupted bouts must not earn an uninterrupted-duration achievement. Total practice and continuous practice should remain separate.
- After illness, a substantial break or recurring difficulty, reassess readiness rather than advancing by date.
- Pain that affects gait, persists or worsens needs appropriate assessment; repeating through it is not the default.
- Stop for chest discomfort, faintness or unusual breathlessness. Seek appropriate urgent help for severe or persistent symptoms. Routine medical clearance is not imposed on every healthy adult beginning moderate activity.

## What supports the design, and what does not

[CDC intensity guidance](https://www.cdc.gov/physical-activity-basics/measuring/index.html) supports using speech as a practical effort guide. Race walking is not automatically vigorous, and conversational practice should not be pushed harder to satisfy an intensity label.

[NHLBI activity guidance](https://www.nhlbi.nih.gov/health/heart/physical-activity/stay-active) supports beginning within capacity and increasing gradually. It does not validate this schedule. [AHA warm-up and cool-down guidance](https://www.heart.org/en/healthy-living/exercise-and-physical-activity/fitness-basics/warm-up-cool-down) gives a 5–10-minute range; the exact five-minute bookends here are a product convention.

The [NHS beginner running plan](https://www.nhs.uk/better-health/get-active/get-running-with-couch-to-5k/couch-to-5k-running-plan/) uses rest days between sessions. Applying that pattern to race walking is a design inference rather than race-walking-specific evidence.

A [randomized novice-running trial](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17940147/) did not find lower injury incidence with a longer program applying the 10% rule. This argues against promising injury prevention from a percentage limit; it does not establish safe race-walking doses or prove abrupt increases harmless.

Coach Dave McGovern publishes a [12-week beginner 10K walking plan](https://www.racewalking.org/walking-10k.html), with an [original schedule](https://www.racewalking.org/pdfs/training/10k-beginners.pdf). It starts with four three-mile days and later adds faster distance intervals. That is materially different from this app's three-day, time-only scope. It is a useful coach comparator, not validation for transplanting its doses.

A targeted search did not locate a validated three-day, time-based beginner race-walking protocol matching these requirements. Published walking plans do exist. No evidence here establishes universal knee safety, rehabilitation benefit or a guaranteed finish time.

## Implementation handoff after review

Add a distinct plan identifier and a separate progression record; never overwrite v1/v2 history. Introduce explicit plan goal-distance metadata before reusing 5K-specific copy. Offer the second program after stage 12 with readiness guidance and an option to keep practising 40 minutes. Keep earlier program history and the ability to return to it. Review longer-session hydration/route/weather guidance and 90-minute native-runtime/battery QA before release.
