# Product contract

Accepted direction: 2026-10-16. Consult the product owner before changing these outcomes.

Help generally healthy adults build toward three weekly exercise sessions and 30 minutes of running or race walking. Completing the planned time is a success regardless of distance or pace. `Build to 30` is a working display name; `zero-to-5k` remains the repository identifier.

The eventual health-oriented target is vigorous aerobic activity. Beginners can start easier; duration and intensity are distinct. Do not count all recovery/warm-up minutes as vigorous, force maximal effort, or position this as diabetes/cardiac rehabilitation.

## Current scaffold

The home screen explains the goal and persists the selected activity. It explicitly states workouts are unavailable. There is no fake Start action, fabricated completion, invented training curriculum, or background reliability claim.

## Future experience

Individual sessions should preview intervals and provide cues alongside external music/podcasts with the screen locked. Repetition is not failure. Running and race walking share infrastructure but require separate technique/progression content and progress records.

Program length can be a preference subject to baseline, availability, and reviewed policies. No universal 20%-weekly increase is accepted; the generator remains a proposal.

Distance, GPS, and performance goals are optional future scope. Thirty minutes is not equivalent to 5 km; GPS cannot certify legal race-walking technique. Never require a pace to earn completion of a time-based session.
